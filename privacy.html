VidFetch privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026
App: VidFetch, by Nico Landman, trading as MarLan Consulting, South Africa
Contact: marlan@mail.grokbot.com

The short version

VidFetch doesn't collect anything about you, and it doesn't send anything to us. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What VidFetch does on your PC

When you paste a link and press Download, VidFetch uses a built-in tool (yt-dlp) to fetch the video from the website the link points to, and saves it to the folder you chose. VidFetch connects to that website, and to the site's own picture servers to show the preview picture. Those websites see that request the same way they would if you opened the link in your browser. Their own privacy policies apply to that.

VidFetch does not connect to any MarLan Consulting server. It doesn't check for updates on its own. New versions come through the Microsoft Store.

What VidFetch keeps, and where

VidFetch keeps a few small files on your PC only, inside the app's own storage:

  • your settings (save folder and the options you ticked)
  • a list of your last 30 downloads (file name, where it was saved, date, quality)
  • small preview pictures for that list

These never leave your PC. Uninstalling VidFetch removes them. Your downloaded videos stay in your save folder until you delete them.

The "Use my Edge login" option

This option is off unless you tick it. Some sites only let you download when you're signed in. When the option is on, VidFetch lets yt-dlp read the website cookies stored by Microsoft Edge on this PC, so the site sees you as signed in, just like in your browser.

  • The cookies are read on your PC and used only for the request to the site you're downloading from.
  • VidFetch does not save copies of them, and does not send them to MarLan Consulting or anyone else.
  • Only use this on your own PC with your own Edge profile. Untick it any time under "More options".

Responsibility

You're responsible for what you download with VidFetch, for having the right to download it, and for following the rules of the websites you use. Our liability is limited. The VidFetch terms of use explain this in full, and they apply together with this policy.

Children

VidFetch isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly handle children's personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
